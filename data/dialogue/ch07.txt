### ch07_boss

HALVRIC [neutral]: I kept order. Every man in this hall does exactly what he must. Nobody panics. Nobody runs.
ROWAN [cold]: Nobody's left.

### ch07_halvric_plea
HALVRIC [hurt]: Wait. Listen to me, boy. You don't know what it was like. Every summer, another village ash.
HALVRIC [sad]: I wrote to the King. I wrote ten times. Someone wrote back and offered me... a firm hand.
ROWAN [neutral]: Then put it down.
HALVRIC [cold]: I can't. I don't remember how.

### ch07_post

NARRATOR: By morning the storm had passed. The soldiers of Dain Hall woke up in their barracks, hungry and frightened.
NARRATOR: Most of them remembered nothing since the spring. Some remembered everything, and those were worse.
DORRAN [neutral]: The garrison's asking for orders, my lord. The town is too. They want to know who holds the Thornvale now.
ROWAN [worried]: Not me.
OSMUND [neutral]: The town council has, strictly speaking, offered you the hall, the seal and the title, my lord.
ROWAN [neutral]: Tell them no. Tell them to pick someone who lives here. I've got a farm.
OSMUND [smile]: "I've got a farm." I'll find a more formal way to put it.
ELISE [determined]: Rowan. Your crest. It's changed. Look at it. There's a second ring.
NARRATOR: The white mark on Rowan's hand had grown. A second ring of light circled the first: the Knight's Crest.
ALDRIC [neutral]: Old Wardens said the second ring was when a crest stopped being a spark and started being a fire.
WREN [worried]: Lord Halvric said "the Chancellor." The King's own Chancellor. Is that... is that treason, to say?
ALDRIC [cold]: Saying it isn't. Doing it might be. Pack for the capital.
