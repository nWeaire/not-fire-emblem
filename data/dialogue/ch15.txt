### ch15_pre
@bg calder_palace
@music tension_low
NARRATOR: For the first time in a generation, three crowns sat at one table in the great hall of Calder.
NARRATOR: King Osric, frail and grey, at its head. The Empress of Dravenhold. Captains of Solmere, in borrowed finery.
SERAPHINE [smile]: Father signed the treaty this morning. With his own hand. It shook, but it was his.
YSOLDE [neutral]: Dravenhold has signed. The captains are arguing about the wine. That means they will sign too.
OSMUND [worried]: My lord. The Chancellor has not taken his seat. He is standing behind the King's chair.
ALDRIC [cold]: Stay near me. All of you. Don't let go of your weapons.
ROWAN [worried]: Father?
ALDRIC [cold]: Just do it, son.

### ch15_reveal
@focus 12,2
NARRATOR: Morvane laid a gentle hand on the old King's shoulder, the way a nurse soothes a sick man.
MORVANE [sad]: Peace. You have all worked so hard for it. I am sorry, truly, that it has to be my peace.
NARRATOR: The King's crest, a small, tired gold thing, rose out of his hand and into the Chancellor's. Osric slumped.
SERAPHINE [hurt]: FATHER!
NARRATOR: Every door in the hall burst open. Grey soldiers came through them, silent, eyes empty, swords drawn.
OSMUND [angry]: YOU! Twenty years I have waited to say it to your face! MURDERER! You murdered the Wardens!
MORVANE [neutral]: Osmund Thorne. You always did ask too many questions, my dear Steward. Sit down.
ELISE [worried]: Rowan, those aren't soldiers anymore. The crest is gone out of them. They're hollow.
ALDRIC [determined]: West door's barred. Hold the hall. I'll get it open. Hold until I do.

### ch15_morvane

ROWAN [cold]: Then you won't mind if I keep trying.
MORVANE [neutral]: I take no joy in it. That is rather the point.
