NARRATOR: Calder was the oldest city in Aldmere. Its walls had stood for six hundred years without once being breached.
NARRATOR: On the morning Rowan arrived, the gates stood open and unguarded, and smoke was rising from the grand square.
OSMUND [worried]: That is the guild street. And that... that is the fountain square. I used to buy my ink there.
KAI [worried]: Why's everyone shouting? Is it a festival? Tell me it's a festival.
DORRAN|ALDRIC [cold]: It's a riot, lad. Bread, taxes, the war talk from the north. Pick one. They've picked all three.
ELISE|MIRA [worried]: Riots don't usually have leaders who stand back and point. Look. There, by the market.
ALDRIC [neutral]: These are townsfolk with axe handles, not soldiers. Don't cut them down if you can help it.
ROWAN [determined]: Then we find whoever's pointing. Get the people clear. We go now.

### ch08_start

NARRATOR: Townsfolk with tools and torches surged round the fountain. The ones who could not fight ran for doorways.
NARRATOR: In each corner of the square, a figure in a plain grey cloak stood a little apart from the crowd, calling out.
TAMSIN [cold]: Four of them. Grey cloaks. They don't throw anything. They just keep the pack running.
MIRA [determined]: And the crowd's going for anyone who can't fight back. We have to get there first.

### ch08_agitator
NARRATOR: The grey-cloaked man fell. His sleeve slid back, and a sigil glowed on his wrist, purple as a bruise.
NARRATOR: Around him, the shouting simply stopped. A baker lowered his rolling pin and stared at it as if it were a snake.
ELISE [worried]: Purple. Like the Binders. But that isn't a crest. It's a copy of one, burned in. A badge.
ALDRIC [cold]: Veiled. They've a hand in the capital now.

### ch08_cloth_merchant
NARRATOR: The cloth merchant had been counting the minutes until the mob broke his shutters. He counted out coins instead.
NARRATOR: "For the shutters I still have," he said. "And for whoever buys a coat from me tomorrow."

### ch08_post_morvane

@music aftermath
NARRATOR: The palace sent for them before the fires were out. A steward in Calder blue led them up a hundred steps.
NARRATOR: The Chancellor met them himself: a grey-robed man with a gentle face and quiet hands, who bowed to a farm boy.
MORVANE [smile]: Rowan of Ashford. I have read your letters, child. Every one. You have done the realm a kindness.
MORVANE [neutral]: The Thornvale, and now our own square. Calder is in your debt, and I do not say that lightly.
ROWAN [neutral]: The men leading the riot had purple sigils. Like Halvric's crest. Someone sent them.
MORVANE [sad]: Yes. I feared as much. There is a sickness spreading, child, and it wears many faces.
MORVANE [neutral]: Rest here. The palace is at your disposal. We shall speak of what can be done.
NARRATOR: Rowan turned to say something to Aldric. The place where Aldric had been standing was empty.
